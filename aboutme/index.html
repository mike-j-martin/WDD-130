Hello faithful reader, please allow me to introduce myself to you.I am married to my wonderful wife rylee, and I own a dog named Bear. Now don't be frightned, he is only 20 pounds and shaped like a football but Bear is my best friend.

I hope to work from home because I have a genetic disease Cystic Fibrosis. For those of you who think I just had an animal run across my keyboard, Cystic Fibrosis, or C.F., is a disease given through genetic DNA. It affects my lungs (making breathing hard), and my stomach (I require pills to eat), and my toilet runs, though I'll spare you those details.

C.F. makes leaving my home difficult, therefore I am attempting to gain a degree in computer programming, so that I may write code for video games. Video games have alwasy been my escape and I want to build these "escapes" for others with similar conditions to my own.

I hope I have helped you learn something new today, and I am excited to learn more about how to code in various languages.
